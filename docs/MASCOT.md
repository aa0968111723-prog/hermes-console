# 龜龜素材來源

使用者提供的 Photo 1 為去背來源，Photo 2 為角色姿態參考。

正式使用：`public/mascot/turtle.png`，1109 × 1419 PNG，具有 alpha 通道；在桌面與手機白底頁面已檢視。

使用內建 imagegen 的 background-extraction 編輯模式。第一版仍有光暈，未採用；第二版保存為專案素材。動畫僅為輕微整体呼吸／晃動／單次彈跳，不宣稱有分層眨眼或揮手。

最終提示：

> Extract the original small full-body seated turtle as a PNG cutout with TRANSPARENT ALPHA BACKGROUND. This is background removal, NOT a new illustration. Keep exactly the original turtle without enlarging/cropping/distorting. All leaves and feet must be visible with 10% empty transparent padding. Completely remove every black background pixel AND every green glow, black halo, and floating speck outside the silhouette. Output only the turtle on actual transparent alpha. Do not render a black/green/white/checkerboard background. Do not add glow. Preserve the original soft green turtle with leaves, eyes, cheeks, belly, hands and shell.
